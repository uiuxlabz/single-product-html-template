Healthy hair is our symbol

From kitchen sink to cult following.

Verdia began in 2023 with one stubborn question: why does "natural" shampoo have to feel like a compromise? Our founder, a biochemist allergic to half the drugstore shelf, started blending botanical bases at her kitchen table.

Twelve rejected formulas later — too runny, too oily, didn't lather, wouldn't smell right — number thirteen earned a cult following among friends, then their salons, then strangers who read about it online. We still make it exactly that way: in small batches, by hand, with ingredients we could name on sight.

  • Formulated and bottled in Portland, Oregon
  • 70% certified organic, the rest fully traceable
  • Never tested on animals — ever
  • Recyclable amber bottles, compostable labels
