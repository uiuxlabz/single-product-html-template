The ritual

How to use Verdia.

Three minutes, three steps — repeated two to three times a week.

01 🚿

Wet & Warm

Rinse hair thoroughly with warm water to open the cuticle and lift natural oils.

02 🧴

Apply & Massage

Work a coin-sized amount into the scalp in slow circles for one full minute.

03 💦

Wait & Rinse

Let it rest for five minutes, then rinse cool for shine and longer-lasting volume.

Wash-day wisdom

A few habits that matter more than the product.

Verdia does its job best when the rest of your routine is gentle too. These are the small things our community swears by.

  • Massage with fingertips, never nails — the scalp, not the hair
  • Twice a week is enough for most; oily scalps can go every other day
  • Rinse with cooler water at the end to seal the cuticle
  • Pat dry with cotton, skip the aggressive towel rub
